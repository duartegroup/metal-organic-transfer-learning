Predicted NAMPT activity for the PACT panel

Every compound in the study, at all three pIC50 thresholds: what the model predicted, how confident the five fold models were, and — where one can honestly be stated — what the answer ought to be. Read before analysing

Almost nothing here has a measured activity

38 of the 39 compounds were drawn or generated for this study — the ablations, fragments and photoproducts do not exist outside it — and NAMPT is not among the 38 targets in the 94-complex training set.

The expected column is therefore filled in only where a basis can be stated and checked, and is blank everywhere else. Each entry is tagged literature, control, chemistry or hypothesis; open a row to read the reasoning. Nothing in that column is an experimental measurement.

The five fold models disagree with each other

Each value is the mean of five independently trained folds whose median spread is about 0.57 at pIC50 > 7. caged_PACT there returns 0.112, 0.119, 0.369, 0.741 and 0.862.

The bar beside every value shows that min–max range, with the mean marked and the 0.5 decision line drawn in. Where the range straddles the line, the yes/no in the predicted column is a coin toss between models, not a result.

pIC50 > 6 does not discriminate

At that threshold the drug, its two halves, tert-butylbenzene and both spectator-ligand controls span 0.0146 in total, and every one of the 39 compounds is called inactive — the model cannot tell an inhibitor from an ancillary ligand. Treat the threshold-6 rows as a negative control on the method rather than as predictions.

Size can move the prediction on its own

The descriptor is a per-atom SOAP mean-pooled over the molecule, so removing atoms can shift the prediction regardless of chemistry. The spectator derivatives measure that axis directly, since they never touch the pharmacophore: at pIC50 > 5 the slope is +0.0003 per heavy atom (r = +0.13, p = 0.81, not significant); at pIC50 > 6 the slope is +0.0123 per heavy atom (r = +0.88, p = 0.02, significant); at pIC50 > 7 the slope is +0.0064 per heavy atom (r = +0.35, p = 0.50, not significant).

The slope is refitted from the geometries in use rather than assumed — it is not a fixed property of the model. Compare compounds of similar heavy-atom count, or subtract the fitted slope where it is real. The n_heavy figure is in the structure column for exactly this reason.

How to read the columns

Structure

Molecular formula and heavy-atom count in the table; SMILES and the full optimized geometry inside each row. Geometries are GFN2-xTB optimized at fmax = 0.05, and the SMILES were perceived from those geometries with RDKit.

For the 26 Ru complexes RDKit has no valence model for the metal, so the SMILES is written as [Ru+2] plus the disconnected neutral ligands. That is a faithful description of the composition but discards the coordination — the Ru–N(pyridine) bond that defines the caged compound is not in it. Use the XYZ for anything geometric.

Activity expected

Blank means no expectation is asserted. Where one is, the tag says on what grounds:

  • literature — reported activity. Only stf_31 qualifies.
  • control — a designed negative control of this study: an ancillary ligand alone, carrying no part of the pharmacophore.
  • chemistry — contains no part of the pharmacophore, or is a fragment far too small to reach the threshold. Near-certain, but not measured.
  • hypothesis — the study's own premise, which the study exists to test. Only caged_PACT. Agreement here is weaker evidence than a control.

Activity predicted & value predicted

The value is the ensemble probability: the mean over five fold models. The boolean is simply that value above 0.5, taken from the model output unchanged. Given the fold spread it is frequently not a meaningful dichotomy — the bar is there so you can see when.

Series

  • reaction species — caged_PACT, stf_31, uncaged_PACT, uncaged_PACT_H2O. These balance atom for atom.
  • spectator derivative — deriv_1…deriv_15: STF-31 and Ru intact, only the ancillary ligands vary, so they measure composition alone. deriv_9 is a byte-identical duplicate of deriv_8 and is not an independent point.
  • ablation — one motif removed from STF-31, free and inside the caged complex. free_M1_pyridineN_to_CH is the anchor: it changes zero heavy atoms, so the size axis cannot explain its result. M1 has no caged form, since the edit deletes the atom that binds Ru.
  • fragment — motifs alone, plus free terpyridine and biquinoline as controls. An isolated fragment is a different molecule, with capping hydrogens and no intramolecular context, so these corroborate rather than prove.
  • photoproduct — what each caged complex becomes after release.
